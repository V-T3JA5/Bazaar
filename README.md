Bazaar is a student-focused community marketplace where college students can buy and sell products directly with one another. Students can list items they no longer need—such as textbooks, electronics, calculators, furniture, bikes, and other useful products—and make them available to the wider student community.

Whether you're a first-year student, senior, or anywhere in between, Bazaar makes it easier to find affordable products from people within your college community. Buyers can view listings, get in touch with sellers through the provided contact information, and arrange the purchase directly.
